# Knowledge Engine / The Memory Vault

**Private research · Local-model experiments**

I'm experimenting with small local language models to turn conversations into source-linked knowledge. The interesting part is measuring whether an extracted idea is actually supported by what was said.

## What I am testing

- Extracting useful ideas and claims from conversations with small local models.
- Keeping enough source context to check the extracted information.
- Comparing an extracted claim with what was actually said.
- Exploring when a proposed memory should remain uncertain or be left out.

Private experiments. I compare extracted ideas with the original conversation and look for unsupported or uncertain claims.
